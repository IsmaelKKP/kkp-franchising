PARTNER & INVESTOR INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to the most common questions about joining KKP Lending.

How much are the franchise fees?

Based on the supplied promotional materials, the Standard Package lists a ₱3,000,000 franchise fee and the Premium Package lists ₱5,000,000. These are franchise fees, not the entire project budget. Final inclusions, payment milestones and obligations are governed by the signed agreement.

Is loanable capital included in the franchise fee?

The promotional materials separately list ₱500,000 in loanable capital for Standard and ₱1,000,000 for Premium. Confirm funding responsibilities, custody and permitted deployment before making a payment.

How much should I budget for the office?

Separate office setup allocations shown in the supplied materials are ₱1,000,000 for Standard and ₱2,000,000 for Premium. Actual rent, fit-out, equipment, staffing and local setup costs may differ.

How do daily collections and revolving capital work?

For the illustrative daily-collection product, a ₱5,000 loan has a ₱125 scheduled daily payment over 80 collection days. Collections include recovered principal and earned interest or charges. Recovered principal may fund additional lending, subject to demand, collections, liquidity and policy. Principal repayments are not profit.

What ongoing support is available?

The described program includes operational guidance, training, marketing frameworks, borrower-screening and collection workflows, and systems support. The exact scope, service levels, charges and responsibilities must be documented in the agreement.

Can I reserve a specific city or territory?

Territory requests are subject to availability, feasibility review, eligibility, any applicable reservation terms and formal approval. Submitting an inquiry does not grant exclusivity or licensing rights.

What income or ROI can a franchisee expect?

There is no guaranteed return. Use the feasibility calculator to review a transparent illustration of monthly collections, recovered principal, earned charges, expenses, provisions, break-even capital and annualized operating results. Actual outcomes depend on originations, defaults, collection performance, overhead, loan pricing, funding costs and local conditions.

What are the main investment risks?

Important risks include late or missed repayments, credit losses, operating cost overruns, insufficient qualified borrowers, liquidity constraints, fraud, regulatory requirements and startup delays. Franchise fees and office setup costs can materially affect investment returns.

Are PSA statistics used to forecast revenue?

No. Philippine Statistics Authority figures are used only as a population reference for the selected location. Borrower penetration, loan performance, costs, interest or charges, and projected returns are separate illustrative KKP assumptions.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

KKP can arrange an orientation, discuss territory availability and requirements, and explain the next steps. The website inquiry form currently requires a dedicated submission endpoint before it can transmit applications.
