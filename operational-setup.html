BRANCH OPERATIONS

Structured for efficient lending operations

Our franchise presentation describes a practical branch layout and defined operating responsibilities, from customer consultation to credit evaluation and disbursement.

Customer lounge

Welcoming area for borrower inquiries, consultation and initial guidance.

Application & credit review

Document collection, borrower screening, assessment and loan approval workflows.

Disbursement & collections

Controlled loan release, scheduled repayment monitoring and collection reconciliation.

Administration & reporting

Records, branch performance monitoring, compliance coordination and operating support.

Illustrative branch staffing

The presentation shows two collection officers, one loan officer, one branch manager, one marketing staff member and two security personnel, supported by an accredited law office. Actual staffing, outsourcing and security requirements depend on the approved location and budget.

YOUR PARTNERSHIP JOURNEY

From first inquiry to branch launch

A clear overview of the proposed franchise partner process. Each stage is subject to qualification, approvals, documentation, and final agreement.

1

Initial inquiry

Submit your contact information, preferred program and proposed territory.

2

Orientation

Attend an online presentation and discuss the business model, fees, risks and requirements.

3

Territory evaluation

Review local population, market demand, competition, and proposed branch location.

4

Application & review

Provide requested identification, KYC and financial documents for evaluation.

5

Agreement & approvals

Review contractual terms, territory conditions, payments and applicable regulatory requirements.

6

Training & systems

Complete operations, credit assessment, collections, reporting and compliance orientation.

7

Branch setup

Prepare the office, staffing, branding, equipment and systems according to the approved plan.

8

Launch & support

Begin operations only after readiness checks and applicable approvals, with agreed ongoing support.
