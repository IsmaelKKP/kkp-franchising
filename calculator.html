How are potential borrowers estimated?

Population × assumed adult share × assumed loan eligibility × (1 − assumed competition) × assumed KKP annual market capture. The calculator then limits daily lending by customer acquisition, available cash and collections. These assumptions can be changed in Advanced settings below.

How the daily lending cycle works

Based on the recurring-collection illustration: the initial 200 loans are released before Day 1, repayments are collected daily, and every complete ₱5,000 available is lent again. New loans start repaying the following day; qualified existing clients may renew after their 90-day term. The cycle continues for 365 days.

All collections stay in the lending pool. Operating expenses are funded separately and are deducted only in the year-end income calculation, not from daily re-lending cash.

Standard scenario: 80% on-time payments, with delayed installments recovered using the assumption in Advanced settings. The ideal scenario is for comparison only.

4. How daily reinvestment works

Issue 200 initial loans → collect scheduled installments daily → re-lend 100% of available collections in ₱5,000 increments → new borrowers begin paying the next day. Operating expenses and funder payments are funded separately from the operator’s pocket and deducted only when reporting annual net income.

This is daily recycling of cash, not a separate daily compounding interest rate. New lending is restricted to available cash; the model never uses a negative balance to fund loans.

5. Estimated 365-day results

Unpaid scheduled installments are tracked as arrears and recovered gradually according to the editable recovery rate. They are not counted as cash until received. Loans issued today start repayment tomorrow.

7. Income after co-funder payments and operating expenses

Income after costs = collected interest − co-funder payments − operating expenses − provision. Recovered principal is not income. These cash-based figures are not audited net profit: unpaid principal, taxes, and other liabilities require separate treatment. The 365-day illustration uses 12 scheduled funder payments at 30-day intervals. Operating costs and funder payments are funded outside the loan portfolio and deducted from annual interest earnings only in the net-income report.

8. What this projection means

Loan disbursements represent lending turnover, not profit. Immediate re-lending assumes continuing qualified borrower demand and enough liquidity to meet obligations. Actual performance depends on collections, defaults, expenses, funding terms and effective-interest compliance.
